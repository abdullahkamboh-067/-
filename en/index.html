Helpful information before requesting a tow

Vehicle details

  • Make, model and approximate size
  • Whether the vehicle starts or rolls
  • Visible damage or locked wheels
  • Low clearance or special loading needs

If your car breaks down

  • Move away from traffic if it is safe
  • Switch on hazard lights
  • Wait in a safe location away from the roadway
  • Share an accurate pickup location

How a quote is prepared

  • Pickup and delivery distance
  • Vehicle type and condition
  • Road and pickup access
  • Requested timing and route
